Research

Nonlinear Analytics

Mathematical foundations of multiscale predictability, forecast-error growth, Logit–Koopman representations, and coarse graining. Which dynamical information does a reduced model preserve, and when can learning distinguish mechanisms that produce similar aggregate behavior?

Predictability and Processes

Weather and climate prediction across interacting time and space scales: coupled ocean–atmosphere dynamics, turbulence closures, subseasonal variability, offshore wind and wind–wave–current analysis.

Hierarchical Data Assimilation

Combining heterogeneous observations and model information across scales: decentralized estimation, learned priors, uncertainty calibration, and digital twins tested for information sharing, physical consistency and prediction under missing observations.

Edge Sensing and Resilience

Environmental sensing and local computation for energy, water, coastal and marine infrastructure exposed to hazards, including quantum-enhanced and on-sensor inference and control approaches, underwater tomographic reconstruction and distributed inference.
